<h2>Thesis supervision</h2>

<p>
I supervise Master's theses in the programs <em>Data Science, Business Analytics e Innovazione</em>,
<em>Economia Manageriale</em>, <em>Economia, Finanza e Politiche Pubbliche</em>, and <em>Innovazione Sociale e Comunicazione</em>.
Since 2022 I have supervised or co-examined more than twenty Master's theses on topics
including NLP applied to news framing, customer satisfaction analysis, biomedical entity matching,
multiclass text classification with green-computing constraints, and topic modeling on tourism data.
A complete list is available in my <a href="{{ site.author.cv_pdf | relative_url }}">CV</a>.
</p>

<p>
<strong>Currently supervising</strong> (selection): F. Tiddia (iTBNB: a modern Naïve Bayes classifier in Python),
G. Ibba (Weakly-supervised lens segmentation for cataract detection — Best Paper Award, IEEE BIP 2025),
P. Marongiu (Time-tracking analysis framework).
</p>

<p>
<strong>Prospective students.</strong> If you are interested in writing a thesis on text analysis,
preference learning, or interpretable classification methods, feel free to email me a brief outline
of your interests together with a transcript of your exams.
</p>
