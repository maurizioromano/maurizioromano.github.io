<p>
I am an <strong>Assistant Professor (RTD-A)</strong> in Statistics at the
<a href="https://www.unica.it/unica/en/dip_economiaeaziendale.page">Department of Business and Economics</a>,
<a href="https://www.unica.it/unica/en/homepage.page">University of Cagliari</a>.
My work sits at the intersection of <em>statistical learning</em> and <em>natural language processing</em>,
with a particular interest in interpretable models for opinion mining and in algorithms for
preference and ranking data.
</p>

<p>
In November 2025 I obtained the Italian National Scientific Qualification (ASN) as
<strong>Associate Professor</strong> in Statistics (13/D1). I hold a Ph.D. <em>cum Laude</em>
in Business and Economics (Quantitative — Statistics) from Cagliari, with a thesis on
<em>General Sentiment Decomposition</em>. Before academia I trained as a computer scientist,
which still shapes how I think about data and methods.
</p>

<p>
Recent and upcoming visits have taken me to Tokyo Metropolitan University (2025),
Universitetet i Oslo (2025), Constructor University Bremen (2023), Jacobs University Bremen (2020),
and Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (2019). In June 2026 I will be Visiting Professor
at the Universidad de Valladolid for PREFSTAT 2026.
</p>
